# Standalone D&D 5e Character Builders, Digital Sheets and Mobile Companion Apps (as of November 2026)

Scope: Demiplane / D&D Nexus, Roll20 Characters (the successor to Demiplane's role for D&D), Dicecloud, Hero's Diary, Fast Character, Tetra Cube, Aurora Builder, Fight Club 5e / Game Master 5e (Lion's Den), Pathbuilder 2e (design reference), plus smaller mobile apps. This feeds a comparison with dnd-sistem, a free, mobile-first 5e web app in Indonesian.

Method note: about 16 search and fetch calls. Third-party app trackers (appgoblin, marlvel.ai, App Pricing Lab) disagree with each other and give no snapshot dates, so treat every rating and download figure as approximate. Items marked "UNVERIFIED" come from prior knowledge or weak sources and need checking on the live store pages.

## Which tools are still maintained in 2026, and which are abandoned or 2014-only?

### Takeaway
The D&D 2024-rules builder space has consolidated around Roll20. Roll20 owns Demiplane, and its D&D 2024 sheet and builder became phone-usable in March 2026. The D&D NEXUS product that launched in November 2025 is a reader and compendium only, with no builder. The older offline-first tools (Fight Club 5e, Aurora, Dungeon Master's Vault) are stagnant or 2014/SRD-only and rely on community-made content files for 2024 material. New indie entrants that advertise "2014 + 2024" support (Hero's Diary, Actos, Character Keep, Fast Character) are where the active development is.

### Cited Findings
**Demiplane / D&D NEXUS (Roll20)**
- Roll20 announced its acquisition of Demiplane on June 4, 2024. At the time, Demiplane and Roll20 "will remain separate tools with separate features and subscriptions". Roll20 said it had no plans to change Demiplane's 5e Nexus, and promised a new D&D 2024 sheet and builder "fully integrated into Roll20 Characters" — [Demiplane blog](https://www.demiplane.com/blog/roll20-acquires-demiplane)
- D&D NEXUS was announced on November 11, 2025. It is a digital reader and compendium for the 2024 and 2014 rules (2024 PHB, 2024 DMG, Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn, Adventures in Faerûn, free Basic Rules for both editions). Staff confirmed: "We do not currently have a D&D character sheet or builder available within the D&D Nexus." Titles owned on Roll20 unlock on D&D NEXUS at no extra cost, and the reverse also holds. Existing 5e NEXUS third-party titles (Grim Hollow, Tome of Beasts, Tales of the Valiant) continue to be supported — [Demiplane forums announcement](https://forums.demiplane.com/t/announcing-d-d-nexus-part-of-the-connected-d-d-experience-on-roll20/5629)
- Wargamer earlier reported that Demiplane's 5e Nexus launched with third-party 5e content rather than official D&D content — [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/demiplane-launches-5e-nexus-third-party)

**Roll20 Characters (D&D 2024 sheet and builder)**
- A March 6, 2026 Roll20 blog post says the full D&D sheet and builder now work on phones via Roll20 Characters. Rolls appear in VTT chat when connected to a game, and there is a compact sheet for VTT-only players — [Roll20 blog](https://blog.roll20.net/posts/dd-sheet-now-on-phones)
- The Roll20 native mobile app was delisted from both app stores. Mobile access is through the browser at roll20.net/characters, and mobile use is free. From a phone you can roll into chat but cannot see maps or move tokens — [Roll20 Help](https://help.roll20.net/hc/en-us/articles/4411213438231-Using-Roll20-on-Mobile-Devices)
- 2014 and 2024 sheets can coexist in one campaign (Jumpgate), but one cannot be converted into the other — [Roll20 forum](https://app.roll20.net/forum/permalink/12299365)

**Dicecloud**
- Open-source (GPL-3.0) "free, auditable, real-time character sheet for D&D 5e", 512 GitHub stars, 2,689 commits. It is hosted at dicecloud.com and can be self-hosted (Meteor, Docker, MongoDB). It is Patreon-funded. The README does not mention 2024 rules — [GitHub ThaumRystra/DiceCloud](https://github.com/ThaumRystra/DiceCloud)
- Version 2 left beta on December 2, 2022, after four years of development — [Dicecloud Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/dicecloud/posts/version-2-date-75207996)

**Hero's Diary**
- The thread was started on September 15, 2025 and edited on November 5, 2025. Hero's Diary is a free web app (heros-diary.com) built for tablets and notebooks, not smartphones. Its library includes 2014 and 2024 SRD items and spells. The developer had "just went public" a few weeks before the thread — [EN World thread](https://www.enworld.org/posts/9755387/); [EN World reply](https://www.enworld.org/goto/post?id=9790396)

**Fight Club 5e / Lion's Den**
- Google Play package com.lionsden.fc5: free, 424K+ installs, 4.31★ from 2.8K ratings, last updated August 20, 2025 — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.lionsden.fc5). Another tracker gives 410.9K installs and 4.35★ — [AppGoblin search result](https://appgoblin.info/apps/901057473)
- One user says Fight Club 5e "hasn't been updated in a long time". The community build scripts include a flag for compiling 2024 content into compendium XML — [FightClub5eXML (lokulin)](https://github.colasdn.workers.dev/lokulin/FightClub5eXML) (mirror)
- I found no official Lion's Den announcement of 2024 rules support.

**Aurora Builder**
- Aurora survives through fan-maintained "elements" content repositories (Aurora Legacy). The project is not affiliated with Aurora Builder or with Wizards of the Coast — [GitHub Azzzzzy/elements](https://github.com/Azzzzzy/elements); [GitHub draxius/elements](https://github.com/draxius/elements)

**Dungeon Master's Vault**
- A review (date not shown) says it is limited to 2014 SRD content, with "no option for creating a 2024 character" — [StartPlaying blog](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/every-character-maker-dnd)

**Other mobile apps advertising 2024 support**
- Actos: character sheet 5e 2024 (iOS) supports both 2014 and 2024 rules — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6745488621)
- DnD 5e Character Keep (iOS) added 2024 rules support and has in-app purchases — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1527271197)
- Book of Spells 5e & 5.5e 2024 (iOS) is a spell reference — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6444626336)
- 5e Companion App (iOS): the listing notes a newer "RPG Companion" app exists and says the 5e Companion App "will continue to operate as usual" — [AppFollow](https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/5e-companion-app/1507254451?country=ro)
- Fast Character: a weak, spam-looking page describes it as streamlined creation "for both 2014 and 2024 rules" with dice rolling, spells, inventory and HP tracking — [scraped page, low reliability](https://sketchy-sync.inkandswitch.com/news/hammytv-nudes). A CUNY course blog also references it — [CUNY Commons](https://dh780fall24.commons.gc.cuny.edu/?p=2490)

### Inferences
- Status matrix (best evidence):
  - Actively maintained with 2024 support: Roll20 Characters, Hero's Diary, Actos, DnD 5e Character Keep. Probably also Fast Character, but that needs verification.
  - Maintained but 2024 support unclear: Dicecloud. It is GPL and active historically, but there is no 2024 statement.
  - Stagnant, with 2024 support only via community XML: Fight Club 5e (last Play update August 2025), Aurora (fan elements).
  - 2014 or SRD-only: Dungeon Master's Vault.
  - Pivoted away from D&D building: Demiplane. Its D&D role is now a reader; building lives in Roll20.
- No dedicated, actively maintained native mobile 2024 builder from a major company exists. Roll20 went browser-only on mobile. This favours dnd-sistem's mobile-first web approach.

## What automation features do users praise most?

### Cited Findings
- Dicecloud automation: changes propagate automatically, so moving armour to another container updates HP, saves and AC — [GitHub](https://github.com/ThaumRystra/DiceCloud)
- Hero's Diary: a "modifier engine", feature tracking with per-rest recovery rules (for example, restore 1 charge on a Short Rest and all on a Long Rest), quick imports from the SRD library, a rules compendium and a rich text editor. Users praised its look and dark mode — [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/posts/9755387/)
- Roll20 2024 sheet: conditions modify the sheet automatically (Incapacitated sets carrying capacity to 0 and reverts when the condition is removed). It supports custom conditions with modifiers (speed, DEX, initiative, Stealth) and resources attached to actions (for example, a Monk ability usable four times per long rest) — [Roll20 blog](https://blog.roll20.net/posts/dd-sheet-now-on-phones)
- Fight Club 5e: auto-calculated stats, prepared-spell and spell-slot tracking, AC that updates with equipment, a dice roller and custom dice calculator, and PDF sheet generation — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.lionsden.fc5)
- Pathbuilder 2e (design reference): users praise it as a character planner that exports a PDF or serves as the in-app sheet, with Android and web availability and a fast remaster update. It is free with ads, and a one-time unlock costs about $5 (Reddit claim). App Store rating is about 4.9, and an aggregator reports about 4.8 from roughly 6K reviews (iOS 4.9, Android 4.7). A Lemmy commenter notes the app and web versions are separate purchases — [Know Direction review](https://knowdirectionpodcast.com/2020/01/the-best-app-for-pathfinder-is-free-pathbuilder-2e-review/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pathbuilder-2e/id6740821064); [marlvel.ai (AI-generated aggregator, caution)](https://marlvel.ai/apps/pathbuilder-2e); [Lemmy](https://mlmym.lemmy.dbzer0.com/post/633377)
- Community recommendations for 5e sheets on mobile include Fifth Edition Character Sheet, which one EN World user said gave "a ton of mileage" on iPhone and Android — [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/best-character-sheet-app.612209/). Roll20 is praised as free, but criticised for subscription-locked features and for not feeling like D&D Beyond — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/fifth-edition-character-sheet)

### Inferences
- The patterns users value most are:
  1. A modifier engine where equipment, conditions and features change derived stats automatically.
  2. Resources with declarative recovery rules (short rest or long rest, partial or full).
  3. Conditions as toggleable modifier bundles that revert cleanly when removed.
  4. One-tap rolling from the sheet.
  5. A guided builder that doubles as the play sheet, with PDF export (the Pathbuilder model).
  6. Free core use with a cheap one-time unlock (Pathbuilder) rather than a subscription (the main complaint about Roll20).
- None of the sources explicitly highlighted concentration tracking. This could be a differentiator for dnd-sistem.

### Gaps
- I found no structured review mining (for example, counts of App Store review themes) for any of these apps.

## How do they handle non-SRD content legally?

### Cited Findings
- **Official or partner licensing (Roll20 / Demiplane):** official 2024 books are sold on Roll20 and D&D NEXUS, and purchases are shared across both. Demiplane obtained permission from Paizo, Darrington Press, Kobold Press and Renegade for free cross-platform unlocks. The announcement does not describe WotC licence terms — [Demiplane blog](https://www.demiplane.com/blog/roll20-acquires-demiplane); [Demiplane forums](https://forums.demiplane.com/t/announcing-d-d-nexus-part-of-the-connected-d-d-experience-on-roll20/5629)
- **SRD plus user import (Fight Club 5e):** the app ships with an SRD 5.1 compendium and an "import from file" feature. Community GitHub projects provide XML source files to compile into compendiums. Fight Club 5e itself is not affiliated with Lion's Den — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.lionsden.fc5); [DnDAppFiles](https://www.github.com/ceryliae/DnDAppFiles)
- **Fan content files (Aurora):** content comes as fan-maintained XML "elements" that are not affiliated with WotC — [GitHub](https://github.com/Azzzzzy/elements)
- **SRD only (Hero's Diary):** ships 2014 and 2024 SRD items and spells — [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/posts/9755387/)
- **SRD only (Dungeon Master's Vault):** 2014 SRD content only — [StartPlaying](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/every-character-maker-dnd)
- **Library subscriptions and homebrew (Dicecloud):** users subscribe to libraries (default libraries are configurable when self-hosting) — [GitHub](https://github.com/ThaumRystra/DiceCloud)

### Inferences
- There are three legal models:
  - (a) Official licence plus paid unlocks: Roll20/Demiplane, D&D Beyond.
  - (b) An SRD core plus a bring-your-own-content import (XML or JSON) that shifts responsibility to users: Fight Club, Aurora, Dicecloud libraries.
  - (c) SRD only: Hero's Diary, Dungeon Master's Vault.
- For a free Indonesian app like dnd-sistem, model (c) plus an optional user import (b) is the realistic path. SRD 5.2 (2024) under CC-BY-4.0 permits translation into Indonesian with attribution. This is from prior knowledge and should be verified against the SRD 5.2 licence page.

### Gaps
- I found no sources on takedowns or legal actions against XML-sharing communities.
- I could not confirm WotC licence terms for D&D NEXUS.
