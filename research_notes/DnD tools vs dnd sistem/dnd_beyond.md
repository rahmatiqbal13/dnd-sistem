# D&D Beyond (Wizards of the Coast) — Incumbent Competitor Profile (as of 4 Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-04. Each fact is cited. "Shipped" means confirmed in the official changelog or a launch post. "Announced" means only promised. Items I could not verify are listed under Gaps.

## 1. Subscription tiers, prices, free limits, content sharing, web vs app pricing

### Takeaway
D&D Beyond is free to start. The free tier allows 6 character slots and can run Maps games with content the user owns. Hero costs about US$2.99/month, or US$2.17/month billed annually. Master costs about US$5.99/month, or US$4.58/month billed annually, and adds content sharing (12 people across 5 campaigns). Books are bought separately, and in-app prices are higher than web prices. The May 2026 "Drops" launch added subscriber content without changing tier prices.

### Cited Findings
- **Free ($0):** up to 6 character slots; can invite friends to campaigns; can create and use homebrew; can run games on Maps VTT; can build a library of official and partnered books. — [D&D Beyond Subscribe](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe)
- **Hero tier (~US$2.99/mo monthly, or US$2.17/mo on the annual plan):** unlimited character slots; monthly new character options; 150+ cosmetic perks; 250+ Maps VTT components; weekly drop-in encounters; early access 1 week before pre-ordered books release; can access and share community homebrew; can host and join games on Maps. — [D&D Beyond Subscribe](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe)
- **Master tier (~US$5.99/mo monthly, or US$4.58/mo on the annual plan):** everything in Hero, plus sharing purchased content with up to 12 people across 5 campaigns; early access 2 weeks before release; homebrew monsters on Maps; uploads of custom maps, stickers, tokens and reveals, with up to 10 GB of storage. A 30-day free trial is offered on paid tiers. — [D&D Beyond Subscribe](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe)
  - Caveat: the subscribe page shows two prices per tier without labels. The monthly vs annual mapping was inferred from the annual checkout links. A semi-annual option exists, but its price is not shown on the web page.
- **App Store in-app prices (US, April 2026 snapshot):** Master Monthly $5.99, Hero Monthly $2.99, Master Annual $54.99, Hero Annual $25.99. — [App Pricing Lab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/1501810129)
- **App Store UK prices (current listing):** Master Monthly £4.99; Hero Monthly £2.49; Master Semi-Annual £25.49; Master Annual £46.99; Hero Annual £21.99. Digital books are £29.99 each in-app (Player's Handbook 2014 and 2024, Tasha's, Xanathar's, Monsters of the Multiverse). — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129)
- **Web vs app:** an App Store reviewer noted that web prices in GBP were lower than the in-app prices. — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129)
- **Drops (launched 7 May 2026):** a subscriber content library, included in Hero and Master. Weekly drops arrive every Thursday and are mostly modular "drop-in encounters"; a larger monthly drop comes on the first Thursday of the month. The Day-1 launch had 500+ items. Hero and Master tier prices are staying the same. — [D&D Beyond: Introducing Drops](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2156-introducing-d-d-beyond-drops-a-growing-content); [EN World](https://enworld.org/threads/drops-will-make-d-d-beyond-subscribers-love-thursdays.719035); [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/05/dd-beyond-unveils-drops-adds-new-feats-spells-and-more-for-every-subscriber.html)
- **Drops and content sharing:** Drops were first excluded from content sharing. A 11 June 2026 update made them shareable through Master Tier content sharing. — [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/dungeons-and-dragons-dd-beyond-announces-changes-to-content-drops/) (as summarised in search results; not fetched in full)
- **Book purchasing complaint:** books must be bought whole rather than in parts. Long-running forum threads ask for "à la carte" (per-subclass) purchasing, and users describe "double paying" because owning the print book does not unlock it digitally. — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129); [DDB forum: Bring back à la carte](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/197218-bring-back-a-la-carte?page=7); [DDB forum: No more buying piecemeal](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/198572-no-more-buying-piecemeal)

### Inferences
- A full digital setup costs about US$30 per core book plus about US$55/yr for Master if a DM wants to share books with the table. For an Indonesian player, one book is a large purchase, since the price is USD-denominated and has no local adjustment (see section 7).
- The free tier is now fairly generous (6 characters, Maps hosting, homebrew). What it lacks is free *content* beyond the Free Rules/SRD. dnd-sistem's opening is free, localized *content* plus mobile UX, not free tooling as such.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the web price of the digital-only 2024 Player's Handbook in USD, which is commonly cited as $29.99 digital or $59.99 for a digital+physical bundle. Only the in-app £29.99 price is verified.
- I found no 2026 change to tier prices beyond the "no change" statement at the Drops launch.

## 2. 2024 rules support (builder, sheet, Free Rules, legacy 2014 content)

### Takeaway
D&D Beyond fully supports the 2024 rules in its builder and sheet, and offers free 2024 "Free Rules" alongside the 2014 Basic Rules. 2014 content stays available and is marked with a "Legacy" badge. The old builder handles the 2014/2024 mix inconsistently, and that is a stated reason for the rebuild.

### Cited Findings
- The 2024 Free Rules coexist with the 2014 Basic Rules in the compendium and serve as a free entry point. They include 16 feats, 48 monsters and 333 spells. — [D&D Beyond: Start playing with the 2024 Free Rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1804-start-playing-today-with-the-2024-d-d-free-rules)
- 2014 classes, species, backgrounds, feats and monsters stay accessible with a "Legacy" badge, and tooltips reflect 2024 rules. Owning the 2014 PHB does not unlock the 2024 versions. — [EN World thread: How D&D Beyond Will Handle Access To 2014 Rules](https://www.enworld.org/goto/post?id=9443302) (secondhand summary of the official announcement)
- Forum users report that 2024 options appear in 2014 campaigns, and that the "2014 Core Rules" source must be chosen manually. One thread is titled "Character Builder Follows Neither 2014 nor 2024." — [DDB forum](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/204888-character-builder-follows-neither-2014-nor-2024) (anecdotal)
- A Feb 2026 Trustpilot reviewer said that after the rulebooks were recreated, previously owned content no longer appeared in character creation, and no discount was offered on the new books. — [Trustpilot](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.dndbeyond.com) (single anecdote)
- The October 2026 changelog added a "Rule Set" filter for Premades and source names on Class cards in Quickbuilder. — [D&D Beyond Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)

### Inferences
- Handling the 2014 vs 2024 mix is a known pain point. An app that defaults cleanly to 2024 / SRD 5.2.1, with a clear and optional 2014 toggle, avoids it.

### Gaps
- I did not find an official 2026 statement on whether the rebuilt builder will keep full 2014 character support. Commenters asked about this on the roadmap post and got no answer. — [Roadmap post](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap)

## 3. 2026 roadmap: platform rebuild, character builder, DM tools — shipped vs announced

### Takeaway
On 19 Feb 2026, WotC announced a three-part 2026 plan: rebuild the game platform as a data-driven rules engine, modernize the Character Builder, and launch DM tools. By early Nov 2026, the shipped parts are Quickbuilder (level-1 only, March), Journals (May, with player journals in July), Scene Prep (Aug 31), and a large set of Maps/combat quality-of-life updates. The full new Character Builder on the new engine has not shipped as of the latest changelog (30 Oct 2026). A new mobile app is planned for early 2027.

### Cited Findings
- **Roadmap post (19 Feb 2026, Brian Perry):** it sets up a public roadmap hub with "Now / Next / Complete" columns and warns that "priorities and scope can change, and sometimes a planned feature may never launch at all." — [D&D Beyond: 2026 Development Roadmap](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap)
- **Game Platform rebuild (Laura Thompson):** the platform is to be rebuilt "from the ground up" as data-driven services, so rules and content can be updated as data. The project has multiple phases, and the new Character Builder will launch on it "later this year." — [Roadmap post](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap); [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/d-d-beyonds-development-roadmap-is-a-complete-rebuild-of-platform.718024/); [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/02/dd-beyond-to-be-rebuilt-from-the-ground-up.html)
- **Quickbuilder (SHIPPED 24 Mar 2026):** described as "a streamlined, art-forward way to build a level 1 character in just a few guided steps." Ability scores and proficiencies are assigned automatically. It is the "first public milestone" of builder modernization, and the legacy builder remains. — [Roadmap post](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap); [D&D Beyond: Behind the Screen – Quickbuilder](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2135-behind-the-screen-the-new-quickbuilder-and-future); [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/03/dd-beyond-launches-new-character-quickbuilder-for-first-level-follies.html); [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/beyond-character-creator-upgrade)
- **Quickbuilder follow-ups (SHIPPED):** works without a DDB account (24 Aug); routes the user to a game or the full sheet after creation (5 Oct); Premade browse refresh with Category/Class/Level/Species filters (19 Oct); Rule Set filter for Premades (30 Oct). — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- **Full Character Builder (ANNOUNCED, not shipped):** "throughout the year" it is to expand to deeper customization, advanced options and multiple creation paths. So far only a concept video and a feedback survey have been shown. The Aug–Oct 2026 changelog has no entry for a new-engine builder. A 27 July entry references the ongoing "game engine rebuild project" without release details. — [Roadmap post](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap); [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- **Journals / session documentation (SHIPPED 27 May 2026):** a feature in Maps VTT. The DM presses "J" for a Quick Jot entry during play, and the Campaign Journal reviews entries across sessions. Shared Player Journals shipped 27 July, and player editing of entries shipped 8 Sept. — [D&D Beyond: Introducing Journals](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2175-never-lose-the-story-again-introducing-journals-on); [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- **Gen Con 2026 announcements (7 Aug 2026):** an all-new mobile app (teaser only; early version planned for early 2027; built on the new rules engine); a rebuilt site search; DM prep tools (scene manager, map pins, bookmarking); "mapless" DM tools for running encounters without the VTT. — [VICE: Every New Tool Announced for D&D Beyond](https://www.vice.com/en/article/dungeons-and-dragons-every-new-tool-announced-for-dd-beyond/)
- **Scene Prep (SHIPPED 31 Aug 2026):** DMs key prep notes to scene pins on maps. `@` tagging to other scenes was added 14 Sept, and the panel auto-opens from 5 Oct. VICE (19 Aug) said the DM tools rollout would continue "throughout the rest of 2026 and into 2027." — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog); [VICE: First Look at Enhanced DM Tools](https://www.vice.com/en/article/dungeons-and-dragons-dd-beyond-offers-first-look-at-enhanced-dm-tools/)
- **Mapless tools (PARTIAL):** a "Theater of the Mind Organizer" map was added to all accounts on 8 Sept 2026 for theater-of-the-mind and in-person play. It is still a Maps-hosted surface. — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- **Combat Tracker:** DMs can change character HP in the Combat Tracker (30 Oct 2026). Monster tokens show AC and condition icons (28 Sept), and bloodied/dead states appear automatically at 50% and 0% HP (17 Aug). — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- **Encounter builder:** not mentioned by name in the roadmap. A commenter complained that it is "too tied to the VTT." — [Roadmap post](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap)
- **No new books on the 2026 roadmap:** Wargamer noted the 2026 roadmap contained no RPG books. — [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/beyond-roadmap)

### Inferences
- In 2026, WotC's DM tooling effort is centred on the Maps VTT (Journals, Scene Prep and the combat tracker all live inside Maps). Mobile-first, in-person play remains underserved until the 2027 app, and that is the clearest window for dnd-sistem.
- The rebuilt builder and the new app are both slipping toward 2027. Users note that features promised for 2025 slipped into 2026 ([DDB forum](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/197218-bring-back-a-la-carte?page=7)).

### Gaps
- The changelog was read only up to the first ~100k characters (latest entries, through 30 Oct 2026). I found no November 2026 entries. Whether the full builder shipped in a beta in early November is unverified.
- I did not confirm a campaign-management overhaul as a distinct shipped feature.

## 4. Maps (VTT) and Project Sigil

### Takeaway
Maps is D&D Beyond's 2D browser VTT. It launched in beta in 2024 for Master tier, and since 16 Sept 2025 free registered users can host with owned content. Uploads and custom assets stay paid. Project Sigil, the 3D VTT, was wound down: about 90% of the team was laid off in March 2025, the end was announced on 24 Oct 2025, and the servers were due to shut down at the end of October 2026. Eligible Master subscribers got a 6-month Master credit.

### Cited Findings
- **Maps free from 16 Sept 2025:** free registered users can run games with maps, modules and adventures they own. Uploading homebrew maps, custom tokens and some tools still needs a paid account, and official adventures require buying the books. The launch was tied to the Heroes of the Borderlands starter set. — [Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/free-maps-vtt); [Bell of Lost Souls](https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2025/07/dd-beyond-to-make-maps-feature-free-for-all-users-sort-of.html); [ComicBook](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/dnd-beyond-maps-vtt-free-dan-ayoub-announcement)
- The current subscribe page confirms that Free can "run games on Maps VTT," Hero gets 250+ Maps components, and Master gets uploads (maps, stickers, tokens, reveals, 10 GB) and homebrew monsters on Maps. — [Subscribe](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe)
- **2026 Maps quality-of-life updates:** Copy/Paste, Polygonal Fog of War, Monster Reveals, 90 new stickers (by Feb 2026); shared dice on Maps and the sheet (23 Feb 2026); Custom Stickers and Custom Token Art (19 Oct 2026, up to 2048×2048 from 23 Oct). — [Roadmap post](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap); [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- Hasbro reportedly said Maps weekly traffic was up nearly 50% since the September (2025) launch. — [EN World forum quoting Hasbro](https://www.enworld.org/goto/post?id=9785824) (not verified against the earnings transcript)
- **Sigil:** the official post "Closing the Chapter on Sigil" is dated 24 Oct 2025 and says: "At the end of October, 2026, Sigil's servers will shut down." Users who accessed Sigil on a paid or trial Master subscription between 1 Mar 2025 and 23 Oct 2025 receive a 6-month Master Tier credit, which pauses an existing paid subscription. — [D&D Beyond: Closing the chapter on Sigil](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2086-closing-the-chapter-on-sigil-and-thanking-the)
- About 30 people (~90% of the Sigil team) were laid off in March 2025, when active development was "sunsetted." A skeleton crew reportedly works with the DDB team to fold parts of Sigil into Maps. — [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/dnd-sigil-vtt-canceled-hasbro-wizards-of-the-coast-2000578128); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/project-sigil-is-officially-dead-and-i-cant-believe-d-and-d-fumbled-its-best-idea-in-years)

### Inferences
- Per the official timeline, Sigil should now (Nov 2026) be offline. Maps is WotC's only VTT, and it is desktop-browser oriented.

### Gaps
- I found no post-shutdown confirmation that Sigil servers actually went dark at the end of Oct 2026.
- I found no official statement on Maps' mobile/phone usability.

## 5. Mobile app (D&D Beyond Player app)

### Takeaway
The current app ("D&D Beyond", iOS id1501810129) is English-only. It has a character sheet that works offline, a dice roller, and an offline book library, and it rates about 4.6–4.7★. Its weak points are parity with the website (no homebrew creation, no piecemeal purchases, no companion management) and partial offline features. A rebuilt app was announced for an early-2027 early version.

### Cited Findings
- **App Store (GB):** 4.7/5 from about 8.7k ratings; version 2.93 released about 6 days before access (2.92.2 on 20 Oct 2026); English only; 321 MB; iOS 17+, plus Mac and Vision. Features listed: a character sheet "online or offline" (HP, spell slots, equipment, proficiencies); dice d4–d100 with themed sets; premades including Baldur's Gate 3 and LMoP; a guided creator; monster stats; an offline library; bookmarks and search. — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129)
- **Review themes:** praise for ease of use, graphics, quick rules lookup and auto-updated books. Complaints: books are sold whole; web-only features (homebrew creation, buying specific elements, companion management); higher in-app prices than web. — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129)
- An aggregator listed 4.6★ from about 123.2K ratings across stores (iOS 2.86.1 / Android 2.87). It is a third-party figure and likely dated. — [Marlvel aggregator](https://marlvel.ai/api/llm/apps/entertainment/com-fandom-playercompanion)
- **Offline limits:** downloaded books open in airplane mode. Some character actions (e.g. hit dice / rests) are reportedly unavailable offline. Staff said offline rests are "on the roadmap... not the immediate one." — [DDB forums (mobile feedback)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-mobile-app-feedback/193640-d-d-beyond-app-on-ios/next) (undated forum posts; may be outdated)
- **Recent mobile changelog entries are only bug fixes:** white bar on the character list (30 Oct 2026), Listings delay (21 Sept), iOS/Android regressions and the roll-visibility setting (8 Sept). — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- **New app:** announced at Gen Con 2026 (7 Aug) with a teaser only. An early version is planned for early 2027, built on the new rules engine, with quick access to the sheet, attacks, inventory and spellbook for remote or in-person play. — [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/dungeons-and-dragons-every-new-tool-announced-for-dd-beyond/)

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the current Google Play rating or review count.
- I did not confirm whether the current app can *create* characters fully (beyond the guided creator) or edit level-ups offline.

## 6. Multiplayer and sync: campaigns, DM view, game log, dice, Discord/Avrae

### Takeaway
D&D Beyond campaigns let a DM see player sheets and share content (Master). There is a real-time Game Log for dice, and shared dice were added in Feb 2026. Avrae, the Discord bot owned by D&D Beyond, mirrors sheet and app rolls into Discord in near real time once a campaign is linked. Game Log reliability was a 2026 fix target.

### Cited Findings
- Free users can invite friends to campaigns. Master content sharing covers up to 12 people across 5 campaigns. — [Subscribe](https://www.dndbeyond.com/en/subscribe)
- **Avrae:** once a DM links a DDB campaign to a Discord channel with `!campaign`, any dice rolled on the character sheet or in the D&D Beyond Player App appear in Discord in real time. Linking accounts lets Avrae import characters without making sheets public. — [Avrae docs: D&D Beyond Content Integration](https://avrae.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ddb.html)
- **Shared dice** on the Maps VTT and the digital character sheet, plus a bag of digital dice for everyone, were announced 23 Feb 2026. — [D&D Beyond posts](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts) (summarised via search; not fetched in full)
- **Game Log stability:** changelog entries describe a refactored Game Log service "to massively reduce game log errors," a fix for runaway websocket connections, and a 15 June 2026 fix for dice results missing from the log. — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)
- Older (2023) forum reports describe Discord roll delays of up to an hour, or missing rolls. — [DDB forum: Campaign game log](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/general-discussion/96124-campaign-game-log) (dated; may not reflect current state)
- Third-party bridges exist, e.g. a Foundry VTT "D&D Beyond Gamelog" module. — [Foundry Hub](https://www.foundryvtt-hub.com/package/ddb-game-log)
- **DM HP control:** DMs can now edit character HP from the Combat Tracker (30 Oct 2026). — [Changelog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/changelog)

### Gaps
- I found no official public API. Third-party tools rely on Avrae or unofficial scraping, and I could not verify the current status of the unofficial character JSON endpoints.

## 7. Localisation, regional pricing, SEA payments

### Takeaway
D&D Beyond is English-only on web and in the app. There is no Indonesian, and translated books are not sold in the DDB Marketplace. I found no evidence of regional (purchasing-power) pricing for Indonesia or Southeast Asia.

### Cited Findings
- The App Store listing language is English only. — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129)
- The French, Italian, German and Spanish 2024 Core Rulebooks are "not for sale on the D&D Beyond Marketplace, or our international D&D Store." They are sold physically in their own markets. — [DDB Support: 2024 Core Rulebooks in other languages](https://dndbeyond-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/46247021698452-Will-the-2024-Core-Rulebooks-be-available-in-other-supported-languages)
- WotC's print localisation languages are English, French, German, Italian, Japanese and Spanish (Spanish also in Latin America about a month later). Portuguese product was halted. — [Wizards: Expanding D&D](https://ddi.wizards.com/news/expanding-dnd); [DDB: Change to supported languages](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1669-change-to-supported-languages-in-d-d-product)
- There is long-standing user demand for a translated D&D Beyond (German, French), and staff have historically said they had no information on plans. — [DDB forum: Translating D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/47203-translating-d-d-beyond-feedback?comment=66); [DDB forum: language options](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/general-discussion/7082-any-plan-to-develop-different-language-option?comment=29)
- An unofficial browser extension ("DnD Beyond Kit") offers interface translations into several languages. — [Mozilla Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/addon/dnd-beyond-kit/)

### Inferences
- Indonesian is not a WotC localisation language even for print, so an official Indonesian D&D Beyond is very unlikely in the near term. This is a durable gap for dnd-sistem.
- Prices are set in USD (web) or by the app-store regional tier (Apple and Google), and no PPP discount was found. A single £29.99 / ~US$30 book is a large cost in Indonesia.

### Gaps
- I could not verify the payment methods accepted on web (card or PayPal assumed, unverified). I found nothing on local SEA wallets (GoPay, OVO, DANA) or on IDR app-store pricing for DDB in-app purchases.
- I did not check the Indonesian App Store listing directly.

## 8. Licensing: SRD 5.2.1 (CC-BY-4.0), Fan Content Policy, 2025–26 changes

### Takeaway
The 2024 rules core is released as SRD 5.2/5.2.1 under CC-BY-4.0, with commercial use allowed. Attribution is the only requirement, and WotC says it cannot revoke it. This legally enables a free Indonesian translation of the SRD content in a third-party tool. Non-SRD book content (most subclasses, monsters, settings) and WotC trademarks remain off-limits.

### Cited Findings
- SRD v5.2.1 is hosted on D&D Beyond under CC-BY-4.0. WotC says it cannot revoke or alter SRD 5.2 or remove it from Creative Commons, and each version keeps its own license. — [D&D Beyond SRD page](https://dndbeyond.com/srd)
- SRD 5.2 was released in April 2025 (article dated 23 Apr 2025). Future errata may produce 5.2.x updates. — [Tribality](https://www.tribality.com/2025/04/23/dd-system-reference-document-v5-2/); [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/new-d-and-d-rules-are-being-given-to-creators-for-free-reflecting-the-lessons-weve-learned-and-the-steps-were-taking-to-rebuild-and-strengthen-our-relationship-with-our-players); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/dandds-new-rules-will-be-available-under-a-creative-commons-licence)
- CC-BY-4.0 allows use, adaptation and commercial publication with attribution to Wizards of the Coast LLC and a license notice. — [TheCrazyGM CC-BY notice (example)](https://thecrazygm.root.sx/legal/cc-by-4-0/)
- The SRD does not cover fan content such as streaming, fan art and cosplay, which falls under WotC's Fan Content Policy. — summarised from [D&D Beyond SRD page](https://dndbeyond.com/srd) via search results

### Inferences
- Translation counts as "adaptation," which CC-BY-4.0 permits. An Indonesian SRD 5.2.1 port with proper attribution is on solid ground. Avoid "D&D"/"Dungeons & Dragons" trademarks in branding (dnd-sistem's name should be checked for this).

### Gaps
- I found no reliable source for the exact release date of SRD 5.2.1 or its diff from 5.2.
- I found no 2025–2026 change to the Fan Content Policy. I did not fetch the policy text.

## 9. User complaints and praise

### Takeaway
Users praise the integrated builder, sheet and compendium, quick rules lookup, and the free Maps change. Recurring complaints are buying content twice (print plus digital), all-or-nothing book purchases, the 2014/2024 builder mix, gaps between the app and the website, slipped feature timelines, and occasional entitlement and checkout bugs.

### Cited Findings
- **Praise:** ease of use, graphics, quick rules lookup, character and monster generation, automatic book updates. — [Apple App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/d-d-beyond/id1501810129)
